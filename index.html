Privacy Policy & Terms

1. Information We Store

NEON ESCAPE operates on an honest, player-first privacy standard. We store only game progression metrics: your chosen callsign/username, permanent Player ID (NE-XXXXXX), high scores, survival duration, achievements unlocked, coins, XP, and cosmetic preferences.

2. Local Storage & Cookies

We use browser localStorage solely to persist your offline runs, volume preferences, and cosmetic loadouts on this device. We do not use third-party behavioral tracking cookies or sell your personal data.

3. Firebase Cloud & Leaderboards

When connected to the internet, high scores and country tags are synchronized with Google Firebase Firestore to power the real global leaderboard. Score submissions undergo mathematical anti-cheat verification to ensure fair competition for all pilots.

4. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat

Scores submitted with anomalous telemetry, impossible survival rates, or manipulated memory values are rejected by sector anti-cheat protocols to protect leaderboard integrity.

5. Monetization Ethics

NEON ESCAPE does not feature gambling, pay-to-win advantages, or manipulative loot boxes. Optional future rewarded incentives and cosmetics are strictly non-advantageous and respect player autonomy.

🍪

Privacy Notice: NEON ESCAPE uses local device storage to preserve your high scores, mission progression, and audio preferences. No third-party tracking cookies are used.
